Delete your account

App: Legit (com.amir.legit) · Last updated: 29 July 2026

In plain words

You can delete your Legit account and everything in it. Do it in the app in about ten seconds, or email us and we will do it for you.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Legit.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed.

Option 2 — By email

Email legit.support@proton.me from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask one question to confirm it is really you. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

Before you delete

  • Cancel your subscription separately. Deleting your account does not cancel billing. Cancel in Google Play or Apple subscription settings first, or you may keep being charged.
  • If you pay for a family group, deleting your account ends paid access for everyone in that group.
  • Deletion is permanent. Scan history cannot be recovered afterwards.
