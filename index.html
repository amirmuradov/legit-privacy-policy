A scam message usually contains someone else's details: a phone number, a sender name, sometimes a photo. When you submit it, we process that content only to give you an answer. Please do not submit content that is unrelated to a suspected scam, and do not submit other people's private information you have no reason to check.

6. How long we keep it

In plain words

Scans stay in your history until you delete them. Delete your account and everything goes.

  • Scan history and shared items — kept until you delete the item or your account.
  • Speed cache — we store a one-way fingerprint of scanned content plus its verdict for up to 24 hours so repeat checks are instant. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into your original message.
  • Account records — deleted when you delete your account, apart from anything we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Your rights and how to delete everything

In plain words

Ask us for a copy, ask us to fix it, or ask us to erase it. Deleting your account is available inside the app and by email.

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to our use of it, or export it. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing — and we do not sell or share your data as those terms are defined there.

To delete your account and data: open the app, go to Settings and choose to delete your account, or email [PRIVACY@YOURDOMAIN] from your account address. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your records is enforced at the database level so that only you and, where you have chosen to share, your family group can read them. No system is perfect. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

9. Children

Legit is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. What Legit is not

In plain words

Legit gives an opinion, not a guarantee. Never move money based only on what this app says.

Legit uses automated AI analysis. It can be wrong in both directions: it can miss a scam, and it can flag something harmless. It is not financial, legal, or professional advice. Always verify anything involving money directly with your bank, using the number printed on the back of your card.
